# Abad Network Institute: Master Proactive Threat Hunting and Become an eCTHPv2 Certified Professional

## Description

Threat Hunting is one of the most vital and high-demand disciplines in modern cybersecurity. As cyber attacks become increasingly sophisticated, traditional reactive defenses like antivirus and automated firewalls are no longer enough. Organizations around the world require skilled Threat Hunters who can proactively search through networks and endpoints to detect hidden adversaries before they cause catastrophic damage.

Global tech giants, financial institutions, defense contractors, healthcare providers, and enterprise SOCs actively seek professionals certified in threat hunting. Demonstrating mastery in threat hunting proves your capability to identify advanced persistent threats (APTs), zero-day exploits, fileless malware, stealthy persistence mechanisms, and covert command-and-control (C2) channels.

My course explains the core methodologies, practical frameworks, and technical skills aligned with the eCTHPv2 (eLearnSecurity Certified Threat Hunting Professional) curriculum to help you understand how to design hypothesis-driven hunting campaigns, analyze complex telemetry, and hunt down hidden threats.

My course is structured into 2 parts:

* the first one is about Threat Hunting Methodology, Frameworks, and Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI). Threat hunting concepts, hypothesis development, MITRE ATT&CK framework mapping, intelligence-driven hunting, baselining normal network and endpoint behavior, telemetry source collection, and logging configurations (Sysmon, Windows Event Logs, Linux Auditd) along with data enrichment strategies are discussed.
* the second part of the course is all about practical Endpoint and Network Threat Hunting along with Detection Engineering. The topics cover aspects like: Endpoint hunting techniques, memory forensics (Volatility, process injection detection, unbacked executable code), Windows artifact analysis (Registry, Prefetch, Shimcache, Amcache, MFT, USN Journal, WMI persistence, Scheduled Tasks), Linux threat hunting, network traffic analysis (PCAP analysis, Zeek/Bro logs, Suricata, DNS tunneling, HTTP beaconing detection, covert channels), threat hunting with SIEM platforms (ELK Stack & Splunk queries), detecting lateral movement, privilege escalation, credential dumping, data exfiltration, and building detection rules using YARA and Sigma.

After going through all the lessons of this course you will have a solid knowledge of how to execute proactive threat hunting operations, investigate advanced adversary TTPs, and prepare for the eCTHPv2 hands-on exam.

With the information here you can:

* work as a Threat Hunter or Senior Cybersecurity Analyst in advanced SOC environments;
* conduct proactive compromise assessments and threat hunting engagements for client organizations;
* work as an Incident Responder or Digital Forensics & Incident Response (DFIR) Specialist; or
* if you are a SOC Manager or Security Director you will know how to establish, mature, and lead a dedicated Threat Hunting program in your organization.

If none of the options above suits your profile you can use the information in my course to deepen your technical understanding of cyber attacks, threat detection, and adversary tactics to significantly elevate your defensive cybersecurity career.

From my course you will get condensed, practical information that you can re-visit anytime you need and after going through this course Udemy offers the possibility to download a certificate for successful completion so you can demonstrate your competence in threat hunting and cybersecurity operations.
